Mika Draws – a connect-the-dots app for kids 3+ that teaches numbers through finger drawing

Kids join numbered dots, hear each number read aloud in the language you choose, and watch a picture appear. No ads, and your child's name is praised at the end of every drawing.

Start free · iPhone and iPad

How to play Mika Draws

1. Pick a drawing

Numbers, letters and more. Each picture shows numbered dots.

2. Join the dots

Your child joins them in order and hears each number read aloud.

3. The picture appears

A praise with the name you typed appears at the end.

What your child learns

Counting and numbers

Joining dots in order practises counting and number recognition.

Letters and languages

Numbers are read aloud in 7 languages: Hebrew, English, Spanish, Arabic, Russian, Chinese and Hindi.

Fine motor skills

Dragging a finger from dot to dot builds control and accuracy.

Confidence

Every drawing ends in success and a personal praise with your child's name.

Safe for parents

No ads

No ads and no pop-ups.

Parent gate

Grown-up areas are protected by a parent check, so children can't get there on their own.

Praise with your child's name

Type the name once, and every finished drawing ends with a personal praise.

Drawing categories

New drawings are added through the year.

Numbers

10 pictures for counting from 1 upward.

Letters

English and Hebrew letters, dot by dot.

Family and greetings

I love you Mommy, Best Dad Ever, Happy birthday.

Special days

Holiday drawings added through the year.

Frequently asked questions about Mika Draws

What age is Mika Draws for?

Ages 3 and up. It is played with a finger and needs no reading.

Can we start for free?

Yes. Download it from the App Store and start drawing for free.

Does Mika Draws teach numbers?

Yes. Kids join dots in order and hear each number read aloud.

Which languages are the numbers read in?

Hebrew, English, Spanish, Arabic, Russian, Chinese and Hindi, in the language chosen in Settings.

Are there ads?

No. There are no ads or pop-ups, and grown-up areas sit behind a parent gate.

Does Mika Draws work on iPad?

Yes. It is designed for both iPhone and iPad.

Does Mika Draws work with Apple Pencil?

Yes. On iPad, children can draw with a finger or with Apple Pencil.

Who makes Mika Draws

Built in Israel by Eli Amar, founder of ELEVATE. Named after Mika, the niece who tested the first versions.
