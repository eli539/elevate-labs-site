Who we are

Kept&Go (“the App”) is provided by ELEVATE, Israel (“we”, “us”). Questions or requests: info@elevate-il.net.

What the App does

You tell the App a reminder — by voice — and it prepares it. At the right time it notifies you and can open WhatsApp, a call or an email with a ready message. You always confirm and you always press send yourself.

Your data stays on your phone

Kept&Go has no accounts and no servers that store your data. Your reminders (the person, what, when, how, the message text and whether it is done) are saved only on your device. We do not receive, see or store them. Because we do not collect your data, there is nothing for us to sell or share.

Permissions the App asks for

  • Microphone and speech recognition: when you record, your voice is turned into text using Apple’s speech recognition built into iOS. Depending on your device and language, Apple may process the audio on its servers under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive the audio.
  • Contacts (optional): the App matches the name you said against your contacts, on your device, so you don’t have to type. Your address book is not uploaded anywhere. You can say no and type a name instead.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled locally on your phone and shown by iOS.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

WhatsApp and other messengers

Kept&Go cannot and does not read your WhatsApp or other chats. It opens WhatsApp (or your phone or email app) with a prefilled message; nothing is sent until you press send. Kept&Go is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta. What happens inside WhatsApp is governed by WhatsApp’s own policies.

Deleting your data

In the App: Settings → Delete all my data. This removes all reminders and settings from your device. Deleting the App also removes its data. If your device is backed up by iCloud, copies may remain in your own backup until it is replaced.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing to access or correct on our side. You may still contact us at any time. We act in accordance with the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 1981 (including Amendment 13) and, where it applies, the GDPR. You may also complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority or your local authority.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy materially (for example, if a future version adds accounts or cloud features) we will update the date above and tell you in the App before the change applies.
