## English (U.S.) — primary
**Name (30):** Kept&Go
**Subtitle (30):** Never forget what matters
**Promotional text (170):** Just say it. Kept&Go finds who, what and when, and reminds you at the right moment with the message ready.
**Keywords (100):** reminder,follow up,whatsapp,promise,voice,todo,callback,tasks,sales,contacts,screenshot,nudge
**Description:**
Kept&Go is made so you never forget who you need to get back to with an answer, and about what — or to try again with the person you called who didn't pick up. We remind you, and manage the reminders you've already managed to forget. You get a notification on your screen when it's time.

No typing needed.

SAY IT
Tap the mic and say: "Remind me Thursday at 9 to send Dana the quote." Kept&Go finds who, what and when.

CONFIRM IN A TAP
Only the parts we're unsure of need your attention — for example, which Dana. Adjust, tick, save.

GET REMINDED AT THE RIGHT TIME
A notification arrives with one-tap actions: open WhatsApp with your message ready, snooze, or mark done.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
Kept&Go never reads your chats and never sends anything for you. You press send.

Available in English and Hebrew. Free. No account — your data stays on your phone.

## App Review notes (paste)
No sign-in is needed. To test: tap "Record reminder" (speak, or tap "Type instead"), confirm, save. Notifications and Contacts permissions are optional; the app works without Contacts by choosing a name manually. The app never reads WhatsApp chats and never sends messages; it opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message after the user taps the action. Data deletion: Settings → Delete all my data (all data is on-device; there are no accounts).

## Must-have in the app before submitting (guideline 4.2 / 5.1.1)
Working local notifications with actions, Contacts, voice capture, Delete all my data, Hebrew RTL + English. (No account, so no Sign in with Apple needed.)
