Mika Draws

Connect-the-dots for kids 3+ · by ELEVATE

Coming to iPhone & iPad

A gentle finger-drawing game for little hands. Children connect the numbers in order, hear each one spoken, and the picture bursts into colour and confetti.

Why parents like it

  • Made for ages 3 and up: big dots, forgiving touch, no reading needed
  • Numbers are spoken aloud, so children learn to count by ear
  • A parent can type the child's name so the cheer says "Well done, Tommy!"
  • 7 languages: Hebrew, English, Spanish, Arabic, Russian, Chinese and Hindi
  • Works on iPhone and iPad, with Apple Pencil support
  • Free. No ads. No in-app purchases. No account. No data collected.
