Who we are

Kept&Go (“the App”) is provided by ELEVATE, Israel (“we”, “us”). Questions or requests: info@elevate-il.net.

What the App does

You tell the App a reminder — by voice — and it prepares it. At the right time it notifies you and can open WhatsApp, a call or an email with a ready message. You always confirm and you always press send yourself.

Local reminder data

Your reminder content is stored locally. No separate Kept&Go account is required. Subscription processing is described below.

Subscription data

Apple processes payments and billing account information. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription access, including for checking free or Pro status. RevenueCat may process a generated app user identifier, purchase and transaction information, subscription status, and technical device or SDK information needed to provide its service. We use this information for entitlement checks, purchase restoration, troubleshooting and subscription administration. We do not send reminder text, contacts or attachments to RevenueCat as part of this integration. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Storage, retention and sharing

Reminders, saved links and attachment copies are stored locally; attachments are marked to be excluded from iCloud backup. Other local data may be included in device backups. When you choose to share, the selected messaging or cloud service receives the content you choose to send and applies its own privacy policy. We do not operate a server-side AI assistant in this release. Purchase records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat as necessary for subscription management and legal obligations. Support emails are used to respond to requests and retained as needed to resolve them and meet legal obligations. Where applicable, processing is based on providing the requested service, legitimate operational interests or legal obligations; service providers may process data outside your country subject to applicable safeguards.

Permissions the App asks for

  • Microphone and speech recognition: when you record, your voice is turned into text using Apple’s speech recognition built into iOS. Depending on your device and language, Apple may process the audio on its servers under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive the audio.
  • Contacts (optional): the App matches the name you said against your contacts, on your device, so you don’t have to type. Your address book is not uploaded anywhere. You can say no and type a name instead.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled locally on your phone and shown by iOS.

We do not use reminder content for advertising or sell personal data.

WhatsApp and other messengers

Kept&Go cannot and does not read your WhatsApp or other chats. It opens WhatsApp (or your phone or email app) with a prefilled message; nothing is sent until you press send. Kept&Go is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta. What happens inside WhatsApp is governed by WhatsApp’s own policies.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all my data removes local app data; deleting the app also removes local data. Device backups may retain copies. Neither action cancels a subscription or erases purchase records held by payment providers.

Your rights

Contact us to request access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights. You may also contact your local privacy authority.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy materially (for example, if a future version adds accounts or cloud features) we will update the date above and tell you in the App before the change applies.
