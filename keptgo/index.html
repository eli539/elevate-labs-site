Never forget what matters to you.

Kept&Go is made so you never forget who you need to get back to with an answer, and about what — or to try again with the person you called who didn’t pick up. We remind you, and manage the reminders you’ve already managed to forget. When the time comes, you get a notification on your screen.

Say it out loud: Kept&Go finds who, what and when, you tap to confirm, and the message is ready to send. (WhatsApp screenshot reading is coming in a later version.)

  • Zero typing — just say it
  • Matches people to your contacts on your device
  • No account, your data stays on your phone
  • You always press send yourself
  • Works in English and Hebrew
